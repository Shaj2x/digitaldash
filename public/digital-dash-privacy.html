Privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Short version: Digital Dash works without an account and then keeps everything on your device. If you sign in, your dashboard is saved to this site's database so it syncs between your devices, and only you can read it. Your data is never sold.

Without an account

Tasks, events, timers, balances, settings and photos you add are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent anywhere.

With an account

  • Sign-in. You can sign in with an email link or with Google. With Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to identify your account. Sign-in links are emailed from hello@digitaldashdaily.com through our email provider, Resend.
  • Your dashboard. Your dashboard data (tasks and project folders, events, courses, balances, spending entries and budget, goals, shopping list, settings and similar) is stored in a database hosted by Supabase in Canada. Database rules make each account's data readable only by that account.
  • Photos. Background and widget images are stored in a private storage area that only your account can access.
  • Reminders. If you turn on reminders, your device's push address and upcoming reminder times and titles are stored so notifications can be sent. They are delivered through your browser's push service (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla).
  • Email reminders. If you turn these on, the notifications you pick (task due dates, the morning summary, calendar events, focus timer, weekly recap and budget alerts) are sent to your account email through Resend. To send them, the message text is stored with the reminder time; it can include task titles, event names and, for budget alerts and the weekly recap, how much you spent. Each email has a link to stop emails.
  • AI helper. Not available right now. If it comes back, this section will say exactly what it sends and when.
  • Gym. Workouts, sets, routines and bodyweight you log in Health are part of your dashboard data: stored on your device, and in your account if you sign in, readable only by you. They are not shared with anyone or sold.
  • Friends. If you turn on Friends, your display name, a friend code, an optional username and profile photo, and your weekly focus time and session count are stored. Usernames are unique, so anyone who types yours can check it's taken and send you a request. Only people whose friend request you accepted (or who you sent one to) see your name, your photo is shown only to accepted friends and to people you sent a request to, and only accepted friends see your focus time. You can hide your focus time, remove friends, and deleting your account removes all of it.
  • Study rooms. When you join a room, people with the same room code see your display name, whether you are focusing and, if you choose, your top task. Room activity is not stored.

Other services

  • Spotify. If you connect your Spotify account, the sign-in tokens stay on your device and are used only to show what is playing and to control it.
  • Last.fm. If you choose to show your live listening, the app asks Last.fm for your recent tracks using the Last.fm username you enter. The username is saved with your settings. Your Last.fm password never touches this app. Stop showing it any time from the same card.
  • App launch list. If you join the list on the home page to hear when the iPhone and Android apps are out, we store that email address and which phone you picked. It's used once, to tell you the apps have launched, and is never shared or sold. Email us and we'll remove it.
  • Fonts. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and some are served from this site under the SIL Open Font License (licences in /fonts).
  • Done on your device. Describe a theme, sound effects and the shopping list's aisle sorting run entirely in your browser. What you type there is not sent anywhere.
  • Sign-in library. The sign-in code is loaded from the jsDelivr content network when needed. Like any website, it receives your IP address to deliver the file.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Cookies and local storage

Digital Dash doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies, and there are no analytics or ad trackers on the site. The app uses your browser's local storage to save your dashboard, settings and, if you sign in, your sign-in session on this device. This is needed for the app to work, so there's nothing to turn off. Clearing your browser's site data, or Erase everything in Themes, removes it.

Age

Digital Dash is meant for people 13 and older. You must be at least 13 to make an account, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you can withdraw consent (for example, by turning off email reminders or deleting your account). Most of this you can do yourself in the app; for anything else, email us and we'll reply within 30 days. Digital Dash is run from Ontario, Canada, and your account data is stored in Canada.

Your choices

  • Download a copy of your data at any time from the account menu.
  • Delete your account from the account menu. This removes your saved dashboard, photos, reminders and devices from the database.
  • Signing out can also clear the data on that device, and Erase everything in Themes clears it without an account.
