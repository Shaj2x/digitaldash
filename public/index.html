How it works

Up and running in a minute

No download, no sign-up wall. It opens in your browser and works like an app.

Open it

Tap Open Digital Dash. A short welcome lets you pick your look and clock straight away.

Add your week

Drop in tasks, classes and workouts. Plan each day and let the clock show what's up next.

Take it everywhere

Add it to your home screen, and sign in if you want your dashboard on every device.

Privacy

Your data stays yours

On your device first

Without an account, everything stays in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Private when it syncs

Sign in and your dashboard is stored so only your account can read it. Delete it any time.

Never sold

Your tasks, grades and workouts are never sold. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything you see is free to use.

How much can I customize?

Nearly everything: 8 themes or your own colour scheme, 12 clock fonts and 12 text fonts, 6 clock styles, photo, gradient and live animated backgrounds, liquid glass, hover animations, corner roundness, the widgets on your home page and even the page titles. Save looks as presets and switch in one tap.

Do I need an account?

No. Open it and start. An account is only for syncing between your phone and computer, and for reminders by email or text.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once it's opened, it keeps working without internet and syncs when you're back online.

Is there an iPhone app?

You can add it to your home screen today and it opens like an app. An App Store version is on the way.
